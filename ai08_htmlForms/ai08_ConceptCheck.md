# ai08 Concept Check

Answer in your own words. (Competencies: 6.4.1–6.4.6)

## 1. What is the mechanism that connects a label to its input? What does that connection give a screen reader user — and what does clicking the label do for a mouse user, for free?

## 2. Radio buttons vs. checkboxes: what's the behavioral difference, and when is each correct?

## 3. What does a fieldset's legend do for a radio group when a screen reader enters it? Why is "Shirt size" as a legend better than a paragraph above the group?

## 4. GET vs POST: which belongs on a form that submits personal information, and what does GET do with the data that makes it wrong for that?

## 5. What is a form's action attribute? What happens today when your form submits (no server), and what WOULD happen on a real site?

## 6. Why do placeholders fail as replacements for labels? Two reasons — one is about screen reader users, one is about everyone.

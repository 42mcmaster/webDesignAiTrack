# ai01 Lesson: About Me Page

You build a page about yourself. It has a title, one main heading, sections, and paragraphs. This is the same page the rest of the class builds in html01.

## Step 1. Decide what to say

Pick three sections. Examples: About Me, My Music, My Goals. For each section, think of two or three sentences. Your words, not Claude's.

## Step 2. Build

First tell Claude:

"Make a folder called ai01_docStructure in my repository folder."

Then tell Claude this. Fill in your own content.

"Make a complete HTML page called aboutme.html. Put it in my ai01_docStructure folder. The title is About [your name]. Put a comment at the top that says built by direction and today's date. The page has one heading level one with my name. Then three sections. Each section has a heading level two and a paragraph. Here is the content." Then tell Claude your section names and sentences.

Ask Claude to read the page back before it saves:

"Read me the structure of the page, top to bottom, in plain sentences."

If it matches what you asked, say save.

## Step 3. Check

Open aboutme.html in Chrome from File Explorer.

1. Listen for the title when the page opens. It should be About your name.
2. Press H four times. You should hear your name at level one, then three headings at level two. If you hear a different order or a different level, tell Claude and have it fix the file.
3. Press Insert plus Down Arrow to read the whole page. You should hear every paragraph. You should not hear the comment. Comments are for people reading the code, not for visitors.

## Step 4. Learn

Tell Claude:

"Read me aboutme.html one line at a time. After each line, say in one short sentence what that line does."

Then ask:

"What is the difference between the title and the heading level one?"

"What happens if a page has no doctype?"

## Step 5. Make it yours

Add anything you want. A fourth section. A list of your favorite artists. Tell Claude what to add and check the page again.

## Step 6. Push

Commit with the summary About Me page. Push.

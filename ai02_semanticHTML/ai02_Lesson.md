# ai02 Lesson: Your Music Site, Home Page

From here on you build one site about your music. This lesson is the home page. It uses the five page regions: header, nav, main, section, and footer. No styling yet.

## Step 1. Decide

1. A name for your site. Example: your artist name, or [your name] Music.
2. Two sections for the home page. Example: About My Music, and Latest Tracks.
3. One list for each section. One will be an ordered list and one an unordered list. An ordered list is for things in an order, like the steps you take to make a track. An unordered list is for things with no order, like the gear you use. Decide which section gets which.

## Step 2. Build

Tell Claude this. Fill in your content.

"Make a page called index.html in my ai02_semanticHTML folder. The title is [site name]. Use a header element with a heading level one that says the site name. Use a nav element with four links: Home, About, Music, Contact. The links can point to the page names index.html, about.html, music.html, and contact.html. Use a main element. Inside main, two section elements. Each section has a heading level two and a paragraph. Section one has an ordered list. Section two has an unordered list. Use a footer element with my artist name and the year. Somewhere in the text, use one strong element for something important and one em element for something I want stressed. Here is the content." Then give Claude your text.

## Step 3. Check

Open index.html in Chrome.

1. Press D to move by region. You should hear banner, navigation, main, and content info, in that order. If a region is missing, tell Claude to fix it.
2. Press H. One heading level one, then two heading level twos.
3. Press L to move to each list. The screen reader says list with 3 items or similar. Press Down Arrow to hear the items.
4. Press K to move through the links. You should hear four.

## Step 4. Learn

Tell Claude:

"Did you use any div elements where a semantic element would be better? List them and fix them."

Then ask:

"What is the difference in meaning between strong and em?"

## Step 5. Make it yours

Add a third section, or more list items, or anything else. Check the page again after.

## Step 6. Push

Commit with the summary Music site home page. Push.
